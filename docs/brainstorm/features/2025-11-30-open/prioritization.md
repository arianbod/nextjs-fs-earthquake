## Implementation Phases

### Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-4)
*Goal: Match competitors, unlock Turkey market*

| Week | Feature | Deliverables |
|------|---------|--------------|
| 1-2 | Turkish i18n | Full Turkish translation, language toggle |
| 2-3 | PDF Export | Professional PDF reports, download button |
| 3-4 | Shareable Results | Public links, OG images, social buttons |

**Phase 1 Outcome:**
- Turkish users can fully use the platform
- Results can be professionally shared/exported
- Viral growth mechanism in place

---

### Phase 2: Differentiation (Weeks 5-8)
*Goal: Unique value proposition*

| Week | Feature | Deliverables |
|------|---------|--------------|
| 5-7 | Retrofit Calculator | Cost estimates by deficiency, ROI display |
| 7-8 | Historical Earthquake Map | USGS integration, interactive map |

**Phase 2 Outcome:**
- Actionable cost guidance (unique in market)
- Educational context for risk understanding
- Clear answer to "what will it cost?"

---

### Phase 3: Revenue & Retention (Weeks 9-16)
*Goal: B2B revenue, daily engagement*

| Week | Feature | Deliverables |
|------|---------|--------------|

| 13-16 | Real-Time Alerts | Push notifications, ShakeAlert/AFAD integration |

**Phase 3 Outcome:**
- B2B revenue stream from insurance/lenders
- Daily engagement through alert notifications
- Professional-grade platform
